## Ringkasan

GLM-5.2 is a flagship model designed for the era of long-context tasks. It supports a truly usable 1-million-token context window-capable of handling project-level engineering contexts in real-world testing-and delivers greater stability in executing long-range tasks and higher reliability in adhering to engineering standards, thereby further increasing success rates in development scenarios. It enables the completion of the entire development lifecycle-from requirements to multi-platform deployable artifacts-in a single task.
