Hostlinku uses prepaid credits. **1 <CreditIcon /> = $1**. Associated API keys share the organization's service-credit balance. Refunds follow the [Refund Policy](/en/legals/refund-policy).

## Top up

1. Open the [Console](https://console.hostlinku.com) → **Billing** → top-up.
2. Choose an amount or preset and a payment method.
3. Select a bank for VA or coin/network for crypto.
4. Check the total, bonus, exchange rate, and fees, then pay as instructed. Credits arrive automatically after verification.

| Method | Provider | Page |
|---|---|---|
| QRIS | iPaymu Direct | Hostlinku Pay, Rupiah QR |
| Virtual Account | iPaymu Direct | Hostlinku Pay, bank VA number |
| Crypto | Heleket | Heleket payment page |

Minimums, maximums, and available methods follow the payment summary. Crypto payment invoices default to **20 minutes**; the provider may extend this. Link lifetime differs from the payment deadline: follow the displayed deadline and reopen the transaction from Billing if the link expires.

A Pay link grants access to one transaction without login. Keep it private. Historical transactions may still have NOWPayments/BlockBee links.

## Usage costs

Prices depend on the model and **key Service Mode**. Check the [model catalog](/en/models), Console **Models**, or `GET /v1/models` with your intended key.

| Dimension | Price unit |
|---|---|
| Input, output, cache reads/writes | Per 1M tokens |
| Web search, when available | Per 1K calls |

Cached tokens are part of input and are not also billed as regular input. Billing uses upstream usage; missing usage may be estimated.

- **Non-streaming chat**: cost appears in `hostlinku.cost`.
- **SSE streaming**: check the final charge in **Logs / Analytics / Billing** after completion.

## Available balance and key limits

`available = balance − reserved`. Some balance is temporarily held for ongoing requests and settled afterward.

Top-ups add organization balance, not a key's **Monthly Spending Limit**. For `402 PAYMENT_REQUIRED`, read the message: top up if balance is insufficient, or wait for reset/change the key cap if reached. See [Authentication](/en/authentication).

## Transactions and invoices

Open **Billing** for transaction history and invoices for paid top-ups. Print or save PDF through your browser. Pay invoices require a valid link and a paid transaction.

The **Billing Profile** is stored in your browser and affects Console printouts. It is not synchronized to other devices or the server invoice; Pay invoices may show different details.

Monitor your Console balance. Auto-recharge and low-balance email preferences are not available in the current Billing flow.

## Historical costs and analytics breakdowns

Charged request totals are stored in logs. Some input/output/cache breakdowns are recalculated at current rates and may differ from historical totals. Changes in displayed breakdowns do not charge old requests again.
