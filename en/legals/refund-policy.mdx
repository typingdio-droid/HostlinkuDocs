## 1. Prepaid model

Hostlinku does not use subscriptions. You add prepaid credit in USD, and the balance is deducted per request based on usage. There are no recurring charges that need to be canceled. This policy is part of the [Terms and Conditions](/en/legals/terms-of-service).

There are two distinct mechanisms that must not be confused:

- **Reservation release**: The balance held for a request is automatically returned to the available balance. No funds are transferred, and no request is required.
- **Refund**: Any unused balance from a top-up is returned to you as cash. It must be requested and verified.

## 2. Automatic Reservation Release

Before a request is forwarded to the upstream provider, the system reserves the estimated maximum cost from the available balance. After that:

- If the request is successful, the balance is deducted by the actual cost, and the remaining reservation is immediately returned to the available balance.
- If a request fails before generating billable output (e.g., an upstream error or timeout), all reservations are released at no charge.
- If a reservation is held due to a system outage, a periodic process automatically releases it and records a refund entry in the transaction history.
- Requests rejected due to insufficient balance (`402`) or rate limits (`429`) are never charged.

Details on costs per request can be found on the console’s [log](https://console.hostlinku.com/logs) page. If you encounter a failed request that remains truncated, report it according to Section 6, including the request ID; approved corrections will be credited back to your balance rather than refunded as cash.

## 3. Refund of remaining top-up balance

A refund for the remaining balance from a top-up can be requested if:

- Requests must be submitted no later than 30 days after the relevant top-up was successfully processed;
- that balance has not been used, or has only been partially used (only the unused portion is refunded);
- The account is not currently suspended or closed due to a violation;
- "Top-up" is not currently subject to a dispute or chargeback.

Usage is calculated based on the earliest top-up first. The amount refunded will not exceed the amount you actually paid for that top-up and may be reduced by processing fees charged by the payment provider or bank that we cannot recover.

## 4. Non-refundable

<Callout title="Notes" type="note">

- balance already used for successfully processed requests, including requests canceled by the client mid-stream;
- top-up bonus, promotional credit, referral credit, and compensation credit;
- balance resulting from manual adjustments by an admin that do not originate from payments;
- top-ups that have exceeded the 30-day limit;
- balance in an account that has been closed due to a violation of the [Acceptable Use Policy](/en/legals/acceptable-use-policy), fraud, or payment abuse;
- third-party costs that have already been incurred, such as blockchain network fees or exchange rate differences.

If the balance is negative, the deficit is deducted first before any remaining balance is refunded.

## 5. Top-up failed or delayed

Your balance is credited only after the payment provider confirms that the payment was successful. If funds are deducted but your balance does not increase:

- Wait for confirmation from the payment provider. QRIS and virtual accounts are typically confirmed within minutes; crypto transactions await the required number of network confirmations.
- If the balance has not been credited after 1 x 24 hours, please send proof of payment (date, amount, method, and reference number or transaction hash) to [halo@hostlinku.com](mailto:halo@hostlinku.com).
- Once the payment is verified, we will credit the appropriate balance or, at your request, issue a refund.
- Crypto payments that are less than the invoice amount, sent to the wrong network or address, or sent after the invoice has expired are handled on a case-by-case basis; funds that cannot be recovered from the network are beyond our control.

## 6. Submission Guidelines

1. Send an email to [halo@hostlinku.com](mailto:halo@hostlinku.com) from the email address registered to your account.
2. Include the top-up transaction ID or related request ID, the amount requested, and a brief reason.
3. For QRIS or virtual account payments, include the destination bank account in the account holder’s name. For cryptocurrency, include the wallet address and its network.
4. We may request additional verification to ensure the submission comes from the account owner and the authorized payer.

<a id="proses"></a>

<a id="7-waktu-dan-metode-pengembalian"></a>

## 7. Return time and method

- We will make a decision on the submission within 5 business days of receiving all required information.
- Approved requests are processed within 7 to 14 business days. The time it takes for funds to be received may be longer depending on the bank or blockchain network.
- QRIS and virtual account payments are refunded via bank transfer to an account in the account holder’s name.
- Crypto payments are refunded in the same asset to the address you provided. The amount is calculated based on the USD value of the refunded balance, minus network fees.
- The refunded balance is deducted from the account when the request is approved.

We may reject submissions that do not comply with Section 3, cannot be verified, are suspected of fraud, or are unreasonably repetitive. Reasons for rejection will be communicated via email.

## 8. Account Closure

Closing an account does not automatically refund any remaining balance. Request a refund first, then request account closure after the refund is processed. Balances in accounts that have been suspended or closed due to violations may not be refunded, in accordance with the [Terms and Conditions](/en/legals/terms-of-service).

## 9. Billing Disputes and Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first before filing a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. We will review it based on your transaction history and per-request logs. A chargeback on a valid transaction may result in your account being suspended until the dispute is resolved, and any dispute fees charged to us may be applied to your account.
