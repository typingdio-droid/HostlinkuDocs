## 7. Pricing and Billing

Costs are calculated **per request** based on the pricing for the model listed on the [Models](/en/models) and [Pricing](https://hostlinku.com/pricing) pages at the time the request is processed, typically per million input and output tokens (including cache tokens where applicable).

1. **Reservation.** Before a request is forwarded to the upstream, we reserve the estimated maximum cost from the available balance. If the available balance is insufficient, the request is rejected with an HTTP status code `402` and no charges are incurred.
2. **Settlement.** After the request is completed, the balance is deducted by the actual cost based on the number of tokens reported upstream, and the remaining reservation is released back to the available balance.
3. **Release.** If a request fails before generating billable output, the reservation is released at no charge. Reservations held due to system outages are automatically released by a periodic process.

Requests canceled by the client during streaming are still billed based on tokens already processed upstream. Under certain conditions, actual costs may exceed the reservation estimate, resulting in a negative balance; this difference is your responsibility and will be deducted from your next top-up.

Price changes apply to requests processed after the changes are published and are not retroactive. Transaction history and per-request costs can be viewed in the console. Prices do not include taxes that may be imposed by authorities or payment providers; such taxes are your responsibility unless otherwise stated at checkout.
