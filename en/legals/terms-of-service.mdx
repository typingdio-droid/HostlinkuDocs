## 7. Pricing and Billing

Fees are calculated **per request** based on the model prices listed on the [Models](/en/models) and [Pricing](https://hostlinku.com/pricing) pages at the time the request is processed, typically per million input and output tokens (including cache tokens, if applicable).

1. **Reservation.** Before a request is forwarded to the upstream server, we set aside (reserve) the estimated maximum cost from the available balance. If the available balance is insufficient, the request is rejected with an HTTP status code of `402` and no charges are incurred.
2. **Settlement.** Once the request is complete, the balance is debited by the actual fee based on the number of tokens reported upstream, and the remaining reservation is released back to the available balance.
3. **Release.** If a request fails before generating billable output, the reservation is released at no charge. Reservations held due to system outages are automatically released by a periodic process.

Requests canceled by the client during a stream will still be billed based on the tokens that have already been processed upstream. Under certain circumstances, the actual charges may exceed the reservation estimate, resulting in a negative balance; this difference is your responsibility and will be deducted from your next top-up.

Price changes apply to requests processed after the changes are published and are not retroactive. You can view your transaction history and fees per request in the console. Prices do not include any taxes that may be imposed by authorities or payment providers; such taxes are your responsibility unless otherwise stated at checkout.
