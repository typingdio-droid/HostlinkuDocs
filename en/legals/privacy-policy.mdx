## 1. Scope and data controllers

This policy applies to the hostlinku.com website, the Console, and the Hostlinku API. The personal data controller is Hostlinku; the business entity’s identity is listed in the [Privacy Contact section](#kontak). This policy was drafted in accordance with Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (PDP Law) and is part of the [Terms and Conditions](/en/legals/terms-of-service).

If you use the API to process your end users’ personal data, you act as the controller of that data, and we process it on your instructions (as a processor) to the extent necessary to operate the Service.

## 2. Data We Collect

<a id="data-akun-dan-autentikasi"></a>

### Account data and authentication

- name, email address, company name (optional), and profile photo if provided by the SSO provider;
- authentication data managed by Keycloak, including password hashes and passkey/authenticator credentials (not the actual passwords);
- account identity from the SSO provider (e.g., Google, GitHub, Discord) when you sign in via SSO;
- email verification status, temporary OTP code, account role, and account status;
- Login session data and security audit logs, such as login and logout times, registration, setting changes, balance adjustments by an admin, and API key activity, along with the associated IP addresses and user agents.

### API key

API key values are stored only as SHA-256 hashes and cannot be reproduced. We store the key name, key prefix for identification, Service Mode, expiration date, credit limit, last usage time, and revocation status.

<a id="metadata-request"></a>

### Request metadata

For requests that pass authentication and balance checks, we log:

- request time, requested model, and API key used;
- the number of input, output, and cache tokens, as well as the billed cost;
- latency, time to first token, streaming mode, finish reason, status code, and error messages;
- the IP address of the request’s origin;
- Internal upstream route information that is only visible to Hostlinku administrators.

Requests rejected before that stage—for example, due to failed authentication, rate limits, or insufficient balance—are not recorded as request log entries in the console and are not charged.

<a id="saldo-dan-pembayaran"></a>

### Balance and payments

We store a history of top-ups, usage, refunds, and balance adjustments, including amounts, methods, statuses, and transaction reference numbers. Payments are processed by iPaymu Direct (QRIS and virtual accounts) and Heleket (hosted crypto). We do not accept or store your full card numbers, CVV, or banking credentials. For crypto payments, wallet addresses and transaction hashes may be recorded.

<a id="data-teknis-dan-komunikasi"></a>

<a id="technical-and-communication-data"></a>

### Technical data and communication

- IP addresses, browser types, devices, and referring pages recorded in server logs and Cloudflare’s protection services;
- Include this information in your correspondence when you contact support, report a bug, or request a refund.

## 3. Request and body logging content

The contents of the request (prompt, message, attachments) and the model’s response are processed to be forwarded to the upstream provider and returned to you. By default, this content is **not stored** in our database; only the metadata in section 2 is stored.

Hostlinku admins can enable **body logging** across the entire platform for debugging and error investigation. While this feature is active:

- The contents of requests and responses are stored along with the associated request logs;
- Fields containing credentials (such as `authorization`, `api_key`, `password`, `token`) and upstream identifiers are replaced with `[redacted]` before being saved. This filtering is based on field names, so any secrets you write in the prompt text are not filtered;
- Content exceeding the size limit is not saved;
- This content is automatically deleted after the specified retention period—14 days by default—while request metadata is retained;
- The content can be viewed by account owners through the console and by authorized Hostlinku administrators.

<Callout title="Notes" type="note">

We do not use the contents of your requests or responses to train the model. Upstream providers process request contents in accordance with their respective privacy policies. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal data.

## 4. Purpose and Basis for Processing

- **Running the Service**: authentication, request routing, and response delivery (fulfillment of the agreement).
- **Billing**: balance reservations and deductions, transaction history, top-up and refund processing (fulfillment of agreements and legal obligations).
- **Security**: rate limit, abuse and fraud detection, CAPTCHA, audit log (legitimate interest).
- **Support and notifications**: account verification, password reset, and service change notifications (contract fulfillment).
- **Service improvements**: aggregate usage statistics for capacity and quality (legitimate interest).
- **Legal compliance**: responding to requests from legitimate authorities and fulfilling tax or accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 5. Data Recipients

Data is shared only to the extent necessary with:

- **Upstream provider model**: receives the request content you send to generate a response. We do not forward your email or account information to them.
- **Payment providers**: iPaymu Direct and Heleket for processing top-ups and refunds.
- **Email provider**: Brevo or Amazon SES for sending OTPs, notifications, and transactional emails.
- **SSO identity providers**: Google, GitHub, Discord, or another provider you choose to sign in with.
- **Infrastructure**: Cloudflare (network, protection, Turnstile, and R2 object storage) as well as server and database providers.
- **Authority**: when required by law or a valid order, or to protect our rights, security, and property as well as those of other users.
- **Business Successor**: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or business transfer, while remaining subject to this policy.

## 6. Cross-border transfers

Some of the recipients listed above, including upstream providers and payment providers, process data outside of Indonesia. We select providers that implement adequate data protection measures and conduct data transfers in accordance with the provisions of the Personal Data Protection Act (PDP Act). By using the Service, you acknowledge that the contents of your requests are processed in the regions where the upstream providers operate.

<a id="7-retensi-data"></a>

## 7. Data retention

- **Account data and API keys**: retained while the account is active, then deleted or anonymized after the account is closed, unless required to be retained for legal purposes.
- **Request and response content**: not stored when body logging is disabled; when enabled, automatically deleted after the retention period (default 14 days).
- **Request metadata and transaction history**: retained for as long as the account is active and thereafter for as long as necessary for billing, accounting, taxation, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention.
- **Security audit log**: retained for as long as necessary for investigation and evidence purposes.
- **Verification and session codes**: automatically expire or are revoked according to the authentication flow.

Backup copies may remain for a limited period after data is deleted from the main system and are not used for any purpose other than recovery.

<a id="8-keamanan"></a>

## 8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures, including HTTPS connections, hashing of passwords and API keys, `HttpOnly` session cookies, rate limits, CAPTCHA on public forms, admin access restrictions, and audit logs. Further details are available on the [Security](/en/legals/security) page. No system is completely secure; in the event of a personal data breach, we will notify you and the relevant authorities in accordance with the provisions of the Personal Data Protection Act.

## 9. Your Rights

Under the PDP Act, you have the right to:

- obtain information about the processing of your personal data;
- access and obtain a copy of your personal data;
- Correct inaccurate data; some corrections can be made directly on the Console profile page;
- request data deletion and account closure;
- withdraw consent or object to specific processing;
- limit or delay processing;
- file a complaint with the competent authority in the field of personal data protection.

Submit a request to [privacy@hostlinku.com](mailto:privacy@hostlinku.com) from your registered email address. We may request identity verification and will respond within the timeframe specified by regulations. We may retain certain data if required by law, such as transaction records. Request a refund of your remaining balance before requesting account deletion.

## 10. Cookies and local storage

- `hl_sid`: server session for Console authentication; `hl_session`: JWT cookie for some workflows. Session cookies have an expiration time and can be revoked;
- SSO state cookie: short-lived to secure the sign-in flow through an identity provider;
- display preference cookies, such as the console sidebar status;
- Cloudflare and Turnstile cookies for protection against bots and attacks;
- Browser local storage for Playground history, profile snapshots, preferences, and Billing Profile. The Billing Profile affects the Console layout and has not yet been synchronized with the server or other devices.

We do not set third-party advertising or analytics cookies. Disabling cookies in your browser may prevent the login and console from functioning.
