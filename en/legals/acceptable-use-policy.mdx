## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every account, API key, and request to Hostlinku, including requests sent by your application on behalf of end users. This policy is part of the [Terms and Conditions](/en/legals/terms-of-service). You are responsible for usage through your account, including by end users of your application.

<a id="diizinkan"></a>

<a id="2-penggunaan-yang-diizinkan"></a>

## 2. Permitted Uses

- conversational apps, assistants, summaries, translations, and text generation;
- search, recommendations, and classification based on model capabilities; embeddings endpoints are not yet available on the public gateway;
- code generation, agent, and workflow automation;
- research, evaluation, experimentation, and prototyping;
- commercial products built on top of the API, as long as they comply with this policy.

<a id="dilarang"></a>

<a id="3-konten-dan-aktivitas-terlarang"></a>

## 3. Prohibited Content and Activities

- **Child exploitation**: sexual or exploitative content involving children in any form. Violations are reported to the appropriate authorities.
- **Violence and Weapons**: incitement to violence, terrorism, or instructions for the manufacture of weapons, explosives, or chemical, biological, radiological, or nuclear weapons.
- **Fraud and social engineering**: phishing, scams, fraudulent investments, document forgery, or deepfakes used to deceive.
- **Malware and intrusions**: creating malware, ransomware, or code to infiltrate systems that do not belong to you or without the owner’s permission.
- **Spam and manipulation**: unsolicited bulk messages, bot networks, fake reviews, or manipulation of metrics and third-party platforms.
- **Privacy violations**: processing personal data without a legal basis; monitoring or tracking individuals without consent; and biometric identification of individuals without permission.
- **Discrimination**: an automated decision that harms a person based on race, religion, gender, disability, or other legally protected characteristics.
- **Copyright infringement**: mass reproduction of copyrighted material or removal of watermarks and ownership marks.
- **Impersonation**: content that misleads by appearing to come from a specific person or organization without permission.
- **Other legal violations**: activities that violate the ITE Law, the PDP Law, or applicable sector-specific regulations.

<a id="teknis"></a>

<a id="4-larangan-teknis"></a>

## 4. Technical Restrictions

- Avoid rate limits, CAPTCHAs, verification, or balance limits, including those involving multiple accounts or API key rotation;
- flooding endpoints, running load tests, or conducting vulnerability scans without written permission;
- Do not attempt to reveal the identity of the upstream, system prompts, or internal configurations that we do not display;
- jailbreak or prompt injection intended to bypass the model’s security policies;
- Use the output to train or fine-tune competitor models if prohibited by the terms of the upstream provider of that model;
- resell, share, or publish API keys.

<a id="aplikasi"></a>

<a id="5-kewajiban-untuk-aplikasi-kamu"></a>

<a id="5-requirements-for-your-app"></a>

## 5. Obligations for your application

- Inform end users that they are interacting with AI if this is not clear from the context.
- Implement adequate human moderation and oversight for high-risk cases such as healthcare, legal, finance, and recruitment.
- Store the API key on the server side and restrict end-user access through your own backend.
- Provide a channel for end users to report harmful output.

<a id="upstream"></a>

<a id="6-kebijakan-upstream-provider"></a>

## 6. Upstream Provider Policy

Each model is operated by an upstream provider that has its own usage policies. You must comply with those policies in addition to this policy. Requests may be rejected or filtered by the upstream provider, and we may restrict access to certain models if the provider requests it.

<a id="rate-limit"></a>

## 7. Rate limit

Each Service Mode has a quota shared across the organization. The Console, public, checkout, and authentication backend endpoints also have limits per IP. Numbers and endpoint coverage are described in [Usage & Limits](/en/usage-and-limits); login protection in Keycloak follows realm settings.

Requests rejected by the limiter receive a `429` status code with no charge; check the `Retry-After` header if available. Limits may vary based on capacity and can be increased for specific accounts; contact [halo@hostlinku.com](mailto:halo@hostlinku.com).

<a id="penegakan"></a>

<a id="8-penegakan"></a>

## 8. Enforcement

<Callout title="Notes" type="note">

Depending on the severity of the violation, we may:

- reject or filter specific requests;
- issues a warning and requests a correction within a specified timeframe;
- limit rate limits or model access;
- revoke an API key, suspend, or close an account;
- Report to the appropriate authorities when required by law or when public safety is at stake.

Balances in accounts closed due to violations are governed by the [Refund Policy](/en/legals/refund-policy).

</Callout>

<a id="pelaporan"></a>

<a id="9-pelaporan-pelanggaran"></a>

## 9. Reporting violations

Report suspected misuse and security vulnerabilities to [halo@hostlinku.com](mailto:halo@hostlinku.com) according to the guidelines on the [Security](/en/legals/security) page. Include a description, the time of the incident, and the request ID, if available.
