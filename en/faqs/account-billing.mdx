## Accounts and API keys

### How do I start?

[Sign up](https://api.hostlinku.com/api/auth2/register) or [sign in](https://api.hostlinku.com/api/auth2/login), then open the [Console](https://console.hostlinku.com). Top up in **Billing** and create a key in **API Keys**. Follow the [Quickstart](/en/getting-started).

### What key settings are available?

Choose a name, **Performance / Low-Cost / Dedicated** Service Mode, **30/60/90-day expiry or never**, and an optional monthly cap. Defaults are Performance and 30 days. There is no scope selector. The key-management API supports additional periods; see [Authentication](/en/authentication).

### How is the secret stored?

The secret is shown once; the server stores a SHA-256 hash. Keep it in your server environment. Keys can be edited, disabled/enabled, and deleted. Replace lost or exposed secrets.

### Are passkeys and 2FA available?

Yes. **Settings → Security** provides WebAuthn passkeys, TOTP authenticators, and recovery codes through Keycloak.

## Balance and payments

### What do credits mean?

**1 credit = $1** as the prepaid service unit. Associated keys share an organization's credits; this is not freely spendable fiat. Refunds follow the [Refund Policy](/en/legals/refund-policy).

### How do I top up?

Open **Billing** and choose an amount and method. New QRIS/VA payments use **iPaymu Direct**; crypto uses **Heleket hosted payments**. Amounts, bonuses, rates, fees, and deadlines appear before payment. Credits arrive after verification. See [Billing & Balance](/en/billing).

### Where are costs and invoices?

**Logs** shows request costs, **Analytics** usage summaries, and **Billing** transactions and invoices. Non-streaming chat includes `hostlinku.cost`; for SSE check the final cost after completion. Missing usage may be estimated.

Print/save invoices as PDF in your browser. The Billing Profile is stored in the Console browser, so Console printouts may differ from Pay invoices or other devices.

### Is auto-recharge or low-balance email available?

Not in the current Billing flow. Monitor and top up before production usage. Adding balance does not increase a key cap.

## Errors and privacy

### What should I do for 429 or 402?

The service quota is **600 requests, 6,500,000 input tokens, and 200,000 output tokens per minute**, shared within the same organization. All organization API keys and members are affected when the quota is reached. See [Usage & Limits](/en/usage-and-limits) for quota details and handling 429.

### Are all failed requests free?

Failures with no billable usage release the reservation. For interrupted streams or partial usage, check the final cost in Logs.

### Are prompts and responses stored?

Body logging is **off by default**; cost, token, status, and latency metadata is still recorded. If enabled by an admin, bodies are size-limited, credential fields are redacted, and retention defaults to 14 days. Secrets within prompt text are not automatically redacted. See the [Privacy Policy](/en/legals/privacy-policy).
