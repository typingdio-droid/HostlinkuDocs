The service quota is **600 requests, 6,500,000 input tokens, and 200,000 output tokens per minute**, shared within the same organization. All organization API keys and members are affected when the quota is reached.

See [Usage & Limits](/en/usage-and-limits) for quota details and handling 429.

## Recommended backoff

1. Use `Retry-After` when present.
2. Otherwise start at 1 second and double each attempt (1, 2, 4, 8 seconds) with jitter.
3. Cap retries, for example at 5 attempts.
4. Queue work in your app instead of sending bursts.

<Callout type="tip" title="Need a higher limit?">
  Contact [halo@hostlinku.com](mailto:halo@hostlinku.com) with your account email and expected load. See [Usage & Limits](/en/usage-and-limits).
</Callout>
