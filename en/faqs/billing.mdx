Hostlinku uses a prepaid balance in U.S. dollars (1 <CreditIcon /> = $1). There are no monthly subscriptions; charges are based on upstream token usage, or an estimate if usage data is unavailable. See [Billing & Balance](/en/billing).

<a id="kenapa-muncul-402-padahal-saldo-ada"></a>

<a id="why-do-i-get-402-with-a-balance"></a>

## Why am I getting a 402 error even though I have a balance?

Check two things:

1. **Available balance**—balances that have already been used or reserved cannot be used again. Check your current balance in the Console → **Billing**.
2. **API key credit limit**—a key may have a daily, weekly, monthly, or total limit. If the limit is reached, requests are rejected even if there is still a balance in the account. Adjust the limit or wait for the reset window.

<a id="bagaimana-cara-top-up"></a>

## How do I top up?

Go to Console → **Billing**. Available methods are displayed on the top-up page (currently QRIS/virtual accounts and cryptocurrency). Minimum/maximum amounts and exchange rates follow those displayed at checkout.

<a id="apakah-pembayaran-langsung-masuk"></a>

<a id="does-payment-arrive-instantly"></a>

## Do payments go through immediately?

The balance is credited after payment is verified. If the status is still pending, check your transaction history in the Console before attempting to top up again. Include the transaction ID when contacting support.

<a id="apakah-saldo-bisa-dikembalikan"></a>

<a id="can-i-get-a-refund"></a>

## Can the balance be refunded?

Unused top-up balances can be refunded in accordance with the [Refund Policy](/en/legals/refund-policy). Bonuses and promotions have no cash value.
